Our Story

The Pink Edit brings block-printed cotton textiles from Jaipur, Rajasthan to Canadian homes. We partner directly with master artisans who have been printing fabrics for generations. Instead of mass production, we import carefully curated collections of the finest block-printed textiles.

Every bedsheet, duvet, towel, and robe in your home is printed by hand using traditional techniques. That's why each piece is unique. That's why they're made to last.

"We don't believe in more. We believe in better."

Our commitment is simple: 100% cotton (no synthetic blends), block printed (not digitally printed), fair partnership with artisans, transparent sourcing, and products designed for quality and longevity.

Why The Pink Edit?
